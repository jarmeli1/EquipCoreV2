# EquipCore Theme Editor Setup Guide

This guide walks you through customizing the EquipCore theme using Shopify's Theme Editor.

## Accessing the Theme Editor

1. Log in to your Shopify admin
2. Go to **Online Store** > **Themes**
3. Find your EquipCore theme
4. Click **Customize**

## Initial Branding Setup

### 1. Logo Configuration

1. In Theme Editor, click **Theme settings** (gear icon)
2. Navigate to **Logo**
3. Upload your logo:
   - Click **Select image**
   - Choose `equipcore-logo.png` or upload a new logo
   - Recommended: High-resolution PNG with transparent background
4. Adjust **Logo width**:
   - Default: 100px
   - Range: 50-300px
   - Adjust based on your logo's aspect ratio

### 2. Favicon Setup

1. Still in **Logo** settings
2. Under **Favicon image**:
   - Click **Select image**
   - Choose `equipcore-favicon.png` or upload a new 32x32px icon
   - This appears in browser tabs

### 3. Color Scheme Configuration

1. Navigate to **Colors**
2. The default EquipCore color scheme includes:
   - **Background**: White (#FFFFFF)
   - **Text**: Dark gray (#333333) - professional and readable
   - **Button**: Trust-building green (#00A651) - for CTAs
   - **Button label**: White (#FFFFFF)
   - **Secondary button label**: Medical blue (#0066CC) - for links
   - **Shadow**: Light gray (#E5E5E5)

To customize further:
- Click on any color swatch to open the color picker
- Use hex codes for precise brand colors
- Preview changes in real-time

### 4. Typography Settings

1. Navigate to **Typography**
2. Configure fonts:
   - **Heading font**: Choose a professional, clean font
   - **Body font**: Select a highly readable font
   - Recommended: Sans-serif fonts for medical/professional look
3. Adjust scales if needed:
   - **Heading scale**: 100-150%
   - **Body scale**: 100-130%

## Section-Specific Setup

### Header Configuration

1. Click on the **Header** section in the editor
2. Configure:
   - **Logo position**: Choose based on your design preference
   - **Menu**: Select your main navigation menu
   - **Enable sticky header**: Recommended for easy navigation

### Announcement Bar

1. Click **Add section** > **Announcement bar**
2. Add professional messaging:
   - Example: "Professional Physical Therapy Equipment - FDA Registered Products"
   - Link to important pages (shipping, certifications, etc.)
3. Style to match brand colors

### Homepage Sections

Consider adding these sections for a professional PT equipment store:

1. **Image banner**
   - Hero image showcasing equipment in use
   - Clear headline about your expertise
   - CTA button to shop or learn more

2. **Featured collection**
   - Highlight key product categories:
     - Knee Rehabilitation
     - Resistance Training
     - Core & Balance

3. **Rich text**
   - Professional credibility statement
   - Certifications and compliance info
   - Why choose EquipCore

4. **Multicolumn**
   - Key benefits (FDA registered, latex-free options, etc.)
   - Use icons for visual appeal

## Best Practices

### For Professional Medical Equipment

1. **Trust Indicators**
   - Add certification badges
   - Include "FDA Registered" where applicable
   - Show warranty information

2. **Clear Product Information**
   - Use product metafields for specifications
   - Highlight material options (latex-free)
   - Show dimensions and weight capacities

3. **Professional Imagery**
   - Use high-quality product photos
   - Include in-use demonstrations
   - Show size references

### Mobile Optimization

1. Preview on mobile devices using the device switcher
2. Ensure:
   - Logo is appropriately sized
   - Text is readable
   - Buttons are easily tappable
   - Product information is accessible

## Testing Your Setup

2. **Test Functionality**

## Saving and Publishing

1. **Save regularly** during customization
2. When ready to go live:
   - Click **Save**
   - Click **Publish** (only if you want changes live immediately)
3. For staged rollout:
   - Save as draft
   - Share preview link with team
   - Publish when approved
