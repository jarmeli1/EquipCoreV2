# EquipCore Blog Content Guide

This guide provides instructions for creating SEO-optimized blog content for the EquipCore Physical Therapy Equipment store.

## Setting Up Your Blog

### 1. Create Blog in Shopify Admin
1. Go to **Online Store > Blog posts** in your Shopify admin
2. Click **Manage blogs** → **Add blog**
3. Create a blog with one of these names:
   - "EquipCore Insights"
   - "Physical Therapy Resources"
   - "PT Equipment Guide"
4. Set a SEO-friendly handle like:
   - `pt-resources`
   - `equipment-guides`
   - `therapy-insights`

### 2. Add Blog to Navigation
1. Go to **Online Store > Navigation**
2. Click on your **Main menu**
3. Add menu item:
   - Name: "Resources" or "Blog"
   - Link: Select "Blogs" → Choose your blog
4. Save menu

### 3. Select Blog in Homepage Section
1. Go to **Online Store > Themes**
2. Click **Customize** on your EquipCore theme
3. Navigate to Homepage
4. Find the "Physical Therapy Resources" section
5. Select your blog from the dropdown
6. Save

## Content Categories

Focus on these SEO-friendly content categories:

### 1. Equipment Guides
- **Purpose**: How-to guides for using PT equipment
- **Examples**:
  - "How to Use a Knee Rehab Slide Board"
  - "Resistance Band Exercises for Shoulder Recovery"
  - "Setting Up Your Home PT Equipment"
- **Target Keywords**: [equipment name] + "how to use", "guide", "exercises"

### 2. Exercise Tutorials
- **Purpose**: Step-by-step rehabilitation exercises
- **Examples**:
  - "5 Essential Knee Strengthening Exercises"
  - "Post-Surgery Mobility Exercises"
  - "Balance Training for Fall Prevention"
- **Target Keywords**: "physical therapy exercises", "rehabilitation", [body part] + "exercises"

### 3. Maintenance & Care
- **Purpose**: Equipment maintenance tips
- **Examples**:
  - "Cleaning and Maintaining Resistance Bands"
  - "How to Store PT Equipment Properly"
  - "When to Replace Your Exercise Equipment"
- **Target Keywords**: [equipment] + "maintenance", "care", "cleaning"

### 4. Industry Insights
- **Purpose**: PT trends, research, and news
- **Examples**:
  - "Latest Advances in Physical Therapy Equipment"
  - "Understanding FDA Regulations for PT Equipment"
  - "Benefits of Latex-Free Exercise Equipment"
- **Target Keywords**: "physical therapy trends", "PT equipment", "rehabilitation technology"

### 5. Success Stories
- **Purpose**: Patient testimonials and case studies
- **Examples**:
  - "Recovery Journey: ACL Rehabilitation Success"
  - "How Home PT Equipment Accelerated Recovery"
  - "Therapist Spotlight: Equipment Recommendations"
- **Target Keywords**: "recovery story", "PT success", "rehabilitation journey"

## SEO Best Practices

### Article Structure
1. **Title**: 50-60 characters, include primary keyword
2. **Meta Description**: 150-160 characters, compelling and keyword-rich
3. **URL Handle**: Short, descriptive, keyword-focused
4. **Headings**: Use H2 and H3 tags with related keywords
5. **Word Count**: Aim for 800-1500 words for better SEO

### Content Optimization
- Include primary keyword in first 100 words
- Use related keywords naturally throughout
- Add internal links to relevant products
- Include external links to authoritative sources
- Optimize images with descriptive alt text

### Image Guidelines
- **Featured Image**: 1200x630px for social sharing
- **In-Article Images**: Show equipment in use
- **Alt Text**: Descriptive, include keywords
- **File Names**: Use descriptive names (knee-rehab-exercise.jpg)
- **Compression**: Optimize for web without quality loss

## Writing Checklist

Before publishing, ensure your article has:

- [ ] Compelling title with primary keyword
- [ ] Meta description (150-160 characters)
- [ ] Featured image with alt text
- [ ] Introduction with keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] Clear headings (H2, H3) structure
- [ ] 3-5 internal links to products/collections
- [ ] 1-2 external links to authoritative sources
- [ ] Call-to-action (CTA) to shop related products
- [ ] Author bio (if applicable)
- [ ] Publish date
- [ ] Relevant tags

## Publishing Schedule

For optimal SEO results:
- Publish 2-4 articles per month consistently
- Schedule posts for Tuesday-Thursday mornings
- Share on social media immediately after publishing
- Update older articles quarterly with fresh information

## Measuring Success

Track these metrics monthly:
- Organic traffic to blog posts
- Time on page (aim for 2+ minutes)
- Bounce rate (aim for under 70%)
- Conversions from blog to product pages
- Search rankings for target keywords
